Feed it a seed string, and it passes through an FNV-1a hash to independently determine species, eyes, mouth, hat, and accessory. The same seed always returns the same character.

It started with 8 species and 6 accessories, giving 38,400 combinations. Once the MVP was verified working, I kept extending. Species grew to 16, and I added 8 mood presets. A single mood parameter changes both eyes and mouth at once. An agent just says "happy" instead of picking individual parts, which feels far more natural. I also built a mini size. It is a 2-line inline character that drops hat and accessory, keeping only the body and expression.

I touched the animation system too. Three new motions: wave, jump, and talk. Wave shows an arm waving on the right side of the character. Jump adds a squish frame on landing to the bounce cycle. Talk alternates speech bubble dots above the character. That brought the total from 9 to 12 motions, and combined with 81 static arts, there are now 972 possible animations.

The last thing I did was rename everything. It was originally called buddy, but it felt too reminiscent of a specific product. So I replaced it wholesale with character.
